Problem

Textile production is difficult to control when information is scattered.

Production Supervisor

"Where is the batch?"

  • • Scattered WhatsApp messages and Excel sheets.
  • • Manual tracking leads to lost information.
  • • Bottlenecks are hard to identify in real-time.

Quality Manager

"Where did the defect come from?"

  • • Lack of traceability per machine and operator.
  • • High rework rates due to late detections.
  • • Paper-based quality logs are easily misplaced.

MYPE Owner

  • • Difficult to make decisions without reliable data.
