Privacy Policy

How Fabric collects, uses, and protects the information of your workshop.

Last updated: November 2026

On this page

1. Information we collect 2. How we use your information 3. Data storage and security 4. Sharing with third parties 5. Data retention 6. Your rights 7. Cookies 8. Changes to this policy

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name, email, phone number, company name, and role in the workshop. We also store the operational data you register in the platform: production batches, stages, quality inspections, defects, machinery records, and downtime events.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve Fabric's services, generate production reports and indicators, send alerts related to your operation, offer technical support, and communicate important updates about your account or subscription plan.

3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls by user role. Passwords are stored encrypted and are never shared. Only authorized members of your organization can view your workshop's information.

4. Sharing with third parties

Fabric does not sell or rent your information. We only share data with service providers that help us operate the platform, such as hosting and email services, under confidentiality agreements, or when required by a competent authority under applicable law.

5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, you can request a copy of your data, and it will be permanently deleted within 90 days, except for records we must keep to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your rights

In accordance with Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733), you can request access to, rectification, cancellation, or opposition to the processing of your personal data at any time by writing to our contact email.

7. Cookies

This website only uses your browser's local storage to remember your preferred language. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools from third parties.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Fabric evolves. When we make relevant changes, we will notify you through the platform or by email, and the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Questions about this document? Write to us at contact@fabric-app.com
